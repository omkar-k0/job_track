Welcome to Laufbahn

A calm job-search assistant for Claude Code, built by a master's student in Germany as an open-source project.

The design follows one principle: fewer, better applications.

What it does

One search, run every weekday

Laufbahn runs a disciplined job search for one person. It is a plugin: it works inside Claude Code (or Hermes Agent), on your own computer.

Find

Looks for roles that fit your hard filters, the ones you set once during setup.

Verify

Checks each role on the employer's own posting, not only on the job board that listed it.

Dedupe

Compares every find against everything you have already touched, so you never apply twice to the same posting.

Tailor

Writes a tailored cover note for the best matches, following your writing rules, and fills in the form.

Track

Follows replies and interviews, and shows you applications that got no answer.

Report

Ends each run with one short daily report that says what needs you.

What it won't do

You stay in charge

  • Send email or LinkedIn messages. It writes drafts only, and a hook blocks every send tool.
  • Submit an application without you. It submits only if you turned on auto-apply during setup, or typed /laufbahn:approve for that role in the same session. Review first is the default.
  • Create accounts, enter passwords, solve visible CAPTCHAs, tick consent boxes, or fill personal-data fields such as passport, nationality, age, gender, marital status and ID numbers.
  • Auto-apply at volume.
  • Send telemetry. No usage analytics, no crash reports, no phone-home of any kind. All plugin data stays on your machine, in ~/.laufbahn.

One thing to know: what you use in a session is seen by your AI provider (for example Anthropic when you use Claude Code) and by any services you connect, under their own policies. Read the privacy policy.

Made for the German market

Werkstudent, Praktikum and first jobs

Looking for a Werkstudent job, an internship or a first job in Germany or the EU? Laufbahn has filters and checks for that market, not just a generic search.

  • Werkstudent
  • Pflichtpraktikum
  • Freiwilliges Praktikum
  • Thesis
  • Part-time
  • Full-time
  • German sources. Bundesagentur für Arbeit, StepStone, XING, Stellenwerk, Absolventa, JobTeaser, Berlin Startup Jobs, and employer boards such as Personio. Searches run in German and English.
  • Filters that fit. Employment type, weekly hours, enrolment, language levels and an hourly pay floor. A posting that cannot fit drops, with the employer's own quote.
  • German applications. A one-page Anschreiben in formal Sie, written in the language of the posting. German forms: it fills Eintrittstermin, Wochenstunden and the CV, and hands you Geburtsdatum, Staatsangehörigkeit and the like.
  • Questions, not advice. Hour limits, internship pay and student work permits are listed for you to settle with the employer, your university or the Ausländerbehörde. It never gives legal, tax or immigration advice.
